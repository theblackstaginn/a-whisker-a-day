# Audio credits

A Whisker a Day currently bundles one audio asset for Yarrow.

## Yarrow purr

- App file: `assets/audio/yarrow-purr.mp3`
- Source recording: **Whiskers' purr edit.ogg**
- Recorder: Adam Cuerden; edited version credited on Wikimedia Commons
- Original source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whiskers%27_purr_edit.ogg
- License: Public domain
- The bundled MP3 is a trimmed, levelled, EQ'd derivative.
- The app fades the recording before its noisy tail so the hiss at the end is not heard.

## Behavior

- The purr plays only when the user directly taps Yarrow.
- Completing rituals, opening settings, toggling sound, or using Yarrow's other interaction buttons does not play the purr.
- The Yarrow purr setting can mute the sound.
- The purr is cached by the service worker for offline use after the app has loaded online once.
