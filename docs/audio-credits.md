# Audio credits

A Whisker a Day bundles only redistributable audio for Yarrow and the optional hearth ambience.

## Yarrow purr

- App file: `assets/audio/yarrow-purr.mp3`
- Source recording: **Whiskers' purr.ogg**
- Recorder: Adam Cuerden
- Original source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whiskers%27_purr.ogg
- License: Public domain
- The bundled MP3 is a processed derivative with filtering, normalization, and edge fades.

## Yarrow meow

- App file: `assets/audio/yarrow-meow.mp3`
- Source recording: **Maullido de gata hembra joven.ogg**
- Recorder: George Miquilena
- Original source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maullido_de_gata_hembra_joven.ogg
- License: CC0 1.0
- The bundled MP3 is a compressed derivative of the CC0 recording.

## Hearth music

- App file: `assets/audio/hearth-music.ogg`
- Track: **Heavenly Loop**
- Creator: isaiah658
- Original source: https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop
- License: CC0 1.0
- Used as an optional low-volume looping background track. Music is off by default and can be toggled in Settings.

## Behavior

- Yarrow's purr and meow respect the existing Yarrow Sounds toggle.
- Hearth music has its own separate toggle and is off by default.
- All three files are cached by the service worker for offline use after the app has loaded online once.
